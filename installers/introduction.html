Control your VST3 plugins with your ElectraOne

These wrappers allow you to have bi-directional control over your VST3s using the ElectraOne hardware MIDI controller.

Each wrapper talks to a specific slot on your ElectraOne.

How to use

  1. On your ElectraOne, load the Preset for the plugin you want to control in a slot.
  2. On your DAW, pick the ElectraOne plugin for the slot you chose.
  3. In the ElectraOne plugin dialog, choose Options and "Scan for new or updated VST3 plugins". This will populate the list with your VST3 plugins.
  4. Select the plugin you want to control with your ElectraOne, and press "Open VST3 Plugin". This will load and show your plugin. It will also analyse your plugin. The top bar of the plugin will say "Restart plugin"
  5. For the plugin to be controllable, restart the plugin or your DAW. The plugin should now be controllable from your ElectraOne.
